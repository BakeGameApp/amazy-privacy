Amazy Privacy Policy

How Amazy processes information when you use the mobile application.

Effective date: October 19, 2026

English

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Amazy processes information when you use the Amazy mobile application. Amazy is a community service for sharing products, store locations and current prices.

2. Information we process

  • Account data: an automatically generated Firebase user identifier and a private device-account identifier.
  • Profile data you provide: display name, username, optional city, avatar and profile settings.
  • Community content: product names, descriptions, brands, barcodes, photos, store names and addresses, map coordinates, prices, availability notes, confirmations and reports.
  • Operational data: synchronization status, timestamps, content identifiers and technical error codes needed to operate and secure the service.
  • Technical data: device type and model, iOS version, and network information such as the IP address used when connecting to our servers or service providers. These data may be processed to provide and secure the service and to measure installations and app usage.

3. Location, camera and photos

Amazy requests location access only while the map is in use to show your position and nearby community finds. Your current device location is processed on the device and is not uploaded as your personal location. Store coordinates that you intentionally publish become part of the public store listing.

Camera and photo-library access is used only when you choose or take a profile or product image. Selected images are uploaded to private cloud storage and are displayed only through Amazy's authorized access rules.

4. How we use information

  • To create and synchronize your anonymous Amazy account.
  • To publish and display community products, stores and prices.
  • To protect the service, investigate reports and moderate abusive or inaccurate content.
  • To maintain reliability, diagnose failures and prevent unauthorized access.

5. Installations and online services

Amazy uses AppsFlyer to measure app installations and openings and process links that open the app. This involves an installation identifier, device and app information, and referral or campaign details. Amazy sends the installation identifier, app identifier, device language and received referral details to ammazzy.com to select the app's starting page. AppsFlyer can access the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking through the iOS permission prompt. If you decline, this advertising identifier is not collected, and the app continues to work. You can change this permission in iOS Settings. Amazy does not sell personal information.

When you access a website through Amazy, cookies and site data may be stored on your device to keep you signed in. Information you submit to the website is processed by its operator under its own privacy policy. Access to the camera or microphone is subject to your device and site permissions.

6. Notifications

Notifications are optional and may include images. Amazy uses Firebase and Apple notification services to deliver them. Firebase processes installation and delivery identifiers. Amazy sends the notification delivery identifier and the identifier of its Firebase service to ammazzy.com, together with your AppsFlyer installation identifier, to register your installation for notifications. When you open a notification, its message identifier and your installation identifier are sent to the same service. A notification may open a website inside the app. You can skip the permission request and change notification permissions in iOS Settings.

7. Public and private information

Your display name, username, optional city, avatar and community publications may be visible to other signed-in Amazy users. Your private device-account identifier is not included in the public profile and is not shown to other users. Reports are visible only to authorized moderators and the person who submitted them where technically applicable.

8. Service providers

Amazy uses Google Firebase for account sign-in, database and notification services, Supabase for protected photo storage, and AppsFlyer for installation measurement and link handling. These providers process information under their own privacy and security terms. Information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence, subject to safeguards provided by applicable law and the providers' contractual commitments.

  • Supabase Privacy Policy

9. Storage and deletion

Information is retained while your account and publications are active or for as long as needed to provide, secure and comply with legal obligations for the service. You can permanently delete your account from Profile → Delete account. This removes your Firebase account, profile, publications, prices, saved items and uploaded photos from active systems. Limited security, fraud-prevention or backup records may remain for a legally permitted period and are not used to restore your public profile.

To request access to or deletion of your personal data, email support@ammazzy.com and describe your request. Include only the minimum information needed to locate the relevant account or installation, such as your Amazy username or account identifier available in the app. Before disclosing or deleting data, we may request additional information only when reasonably necessary to verify that the data belongs to you. Verified requests are handled within the time limits required by applicable law.

10. Security and your choices

Amazy uses restricted access and protected storage to help keep information secure. No system can guarantee absolute security. You may edit public profile information in the app, deny optional camera, photo or location permissions in iOS Settings, report community content, block abusive authors, or delete your account. Blocking immediately hides that author's content only from your personal feed and sends a report to moderation; it does not hide the content from other users unless moderation takes action.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional rights to access, correct, restrict, object to, export or erase personal information and to complain to a data-protection authority.

11. Children

Amazy is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum digital-consent age required in their country. If you believe a child provided personal information without valid authorization, use the support contact described below.

12. Contact and policy changes

For privacy requests, questions or requests to exercise your data rights, contact the Amazy developer at support@ammazzy.com. If this policy changes materially, the effective date below will be updated and the revised policy will be made available in the app and on this page.
